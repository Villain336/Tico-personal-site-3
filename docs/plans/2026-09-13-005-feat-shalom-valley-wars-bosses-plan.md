# Shalom Valley — Raids, Wars, Baal, Moloch, Dragon

## Goal Capsule

- **Objective:** Finish Era 4 after Goliath: bannered raid nights, a mustered siege, and the three named bosses behind him.
- **Out of scope:** Divine Judgment, God as a unit, Jesus as a recruit, a second away-mission map.

## Locked slice

1. After Goliath falls, nights can become **raids** (more shadows + a captain). Not two raids in a row.
2. Killing a captain reveals the idol city. At the hall, **Muster** (conscription + 40 coins from bank then purse) books a **siege** night.
3. Boss order: Goliath → Baal → Moloch → the dragon. One named boss at a time. They stay through dawn.
4. Named drops: raid banner, Baal's censer, Moloch's brand, dragon scale.
5. God is never this dragon. Jesus stays reserved.
