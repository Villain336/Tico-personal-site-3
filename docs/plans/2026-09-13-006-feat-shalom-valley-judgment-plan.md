# Shalom Valley — Divine Judgment

## Goal Capsule

- **Objective:** Finish Era 5: signs in the land, a persistent Book of named souls, and a telegraphed verdict. Jesus is the capstone recruit. God is never a unit.
- **Out of scope:** God as a player or enemy, Jesus as a fighter, a second map, rewriting landmarks or `MAP_SEED`.

## Locked slice

1. After day 4, a valley with people (or standing idols) can receive a **sign** at dawn: drought, a long night, or a quiet dawn. Never two signs in a row.
2. The first sign calls **Jesus** to the Oaks of Mamre. He is recruited, he walks the valley, he does not take a sword.
3. His quest: witness signs or show mercy — twice. Turning him in books **Judgment at the next dawn**.
4. The **Book** keeps named souls (arrived, fallen, redeemed, judged). Letters quote it. Open it with the books (L).
5. The verdict weighs sin, loyalty, mercy, exile, idols, blessing, and Jesus. Outcomes: blessing, exile of the wicked, or a tear-back of the outer dark. Presence is weather, voice, and verdict.
